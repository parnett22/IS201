The Rise of Power Hitting in Major League Baseball

How launch angle, exit velocity, analytics, and modern swing design changed the game.

Why Power Hitting Took Over

Baseball has always valued home runs, but the modern game has become increasingly focused on damage: hitting the ball hard, in the air, and for extra bases.

  1. Major causes of the power surge
    • More emphasis on launch angle
    • More use of bat-speed and swing-plane data
    • More hitters accepting strikeouts in exchange for power
    • More front offices valuing slugging percentage and OPS
  2. Effects on the modern game
    • More home runs
    • More strikeouts
    • More highlight-driven offensive production
    • More focus on exit velocity and barrel rate
